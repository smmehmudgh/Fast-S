## 🌟 What is Fast S?

**Fast S** is a beautifully crafted, real-time chat application that brings people together instantly. Whether it's a quick message to a friend or a group conversation with your squad — Fast S makes every chat feel alive.

> 💡 **Simple. Fast. Beautiful.** No clutter, no lag, just pure conversation.

## ✨ Features

<br>

### 💬 **Real-Time Messaging**

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

- ⚡ **Instant delivery** — messages appear the moment they're sent
- ✓✓ **Read receipts** — know exactly when your message was seen
- ✍️ **Typing indicator** — watch the dots dance while someone types
- 🟢 **Online status** — see who's available right now
- 🕐 **Smart timestamps** — "just now", "2m ago", "yesterday"

</td>
<td width="50%">

- ↩️ **Reply** to any message
- ✏️ **Edit** your sent messages
- 🗑️ **Delete** for yourself or for everyone
- ↪️ **Forward** to any chat instantly
- ⭐ **Star** messages you want to keep

</td>
</tr>
</table>

<br>

### 😍 **Expressive Reactions**

React to any message with a single tap — no emoji picker needed.

| ❤️ | 😂 | 👍 | 😮 | 😢 | 🙏 |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Love | Laugh | Like | Wow | Sad | Thanks |

</div>

<br>

### 👥 **Friends & Social**

- 🔍 **Smart search** — find people by name or `@username`
- ➕ **Friend requests** — send, accept, or politely decline
- 🔔 **Instant notifications** — never miss a request
- 🚫 **Block & report** — full control over your space
- ❌ **Unfriend** with a safety confirmation

<br>

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Group Chats**

Bring up to **5 people** into a single conversation.

- 🎉 Create groups in seconds
- 👑 Admin controls for group management
- 🖼️ Custom group avatars
- 💬 Same rich features as direct chats

<br>

### 📎 **Rich Media Sharing**
